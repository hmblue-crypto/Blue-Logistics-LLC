FAST PREP

Information that helps us quote faster.

Good freight data reduces back-and-forth and helps the team match the shipment with the right mode and equipment.

Lane

Exact pickup and delivery city/ZIP, plus any appointment or facility restrictions.

Freight

Commodity, total weight, dimensions, pallet/piece count, stackability and special handling.

Timing

Ready date, pickup window, delivery requirement, urgency and whether dates are flexible.
