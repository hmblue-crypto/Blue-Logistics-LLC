EQUIPMENT GUIDE

Choosing your equipment

Dry van is common for standard enclosed freight. Reefer supports temperature-controlled shipments. Flatbed or other open-deck equipment may fit freight with unusual dimensions or loading needs. Smaller, time-sensitive loads may need a cargo van or box truck.

Share freight dimensions, total weight, temperature requirements, loading method and pickup/delivery restrictions so the right equipment can be evaluated.

LTL BASICS

What matters for LTL?

Less-than-truckload service can fit smaller palletized shipments sharing trailer space. Accurate dimensions, weight, commodity, packaging and freight class help prevent avoidable reclassification and handling problems.

Also identify residential or limited-access facilities, liftgate requirements, delivery appointments and whether freight can be safely stacked.

ACCESSORIAL GLOSSARY

Common services and charges

Liftgate: loading or unloading where no dock is available. Limited access: service at a restricted or nonstandard location. Detention: extra carrier waiting time beyond the agreed allowance. Layover: an extended delay affecting the scheduled move.

Inside delivery and appointments may also change the service requirements. Always disclose these needs before a quote is finalized.

IMPORT HANDOFF GUIDE

Port to inland delivery

For port-connected freight, send the terminal or port location, inland delivery address, cargo type, container information where relevant, available release/availability dates, and warehouse or receiving instructions.

Clarify the parties handling customs, terminal release, drayage and storage before a move is booked. Blue Logistics can discuss the domestic transportation scope.
