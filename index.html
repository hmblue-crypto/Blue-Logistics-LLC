QUICK ANSWERS

Freight does not have to be complicated.

What information do I need for a quote?

Pickup location, delivery location, what you are shipping, approximate weight and when it needs to move. Send what you know and we can help with the rest.

What if I do not know whether it is LTL or FTL?

That is fine. Choose the closest option or “Help me choose.” Blue Logistics can help determine the appropriate mode and equipment.

Can I track a shipment online?

Yes. Existing customers can use the Customer Portal for available shipment information and documents.

Do I need an account before requesting a quote?

No. New customers can request freight pricing without creating an account first.
