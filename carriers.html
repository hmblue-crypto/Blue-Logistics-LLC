HOW IT WORKS

A clear carrier onboarding path.

1Submit Interest

Send company, contact, authority and equipment information securely.

2Verify

Blue Logistics reviews operating authority, insurance and safety information.

3Complete Setup

Provide required carrier packet documents and payment information through approved channels.

4Book Freight

Once approved, eligible carriers can be considered for matching freight opportunities.

COMMON REQUIREMENTS

What to have ready

  • Active MC / USDOT authority
  • Current insurance certificates
  • W-9 / company information
  • Dispatcher and after-hours contacts
  • Equipment and operating region
WHAT WE VALUE

Strong carrier partnerships

  • On-time pickup and delivery
  • Proactive status updates
  • Accurate POD / BOL paperwork
  • Professional shipper communication
  • Immediate exception reporting
