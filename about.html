QUICK ANSWERS

Before you request a quote.

What does Blue Logistics move?

Blue Logistics supports domestic LTL and FTL freight, import-connected transportation, warehousing and a range of equipment needs depending on the shipment.

Do I need to know the freight class or equipment?

No. Send the pickup, delivery, commodity, approximate weight and timing you know. We can help determine the next details.

Can I verify Blue Logistics' broker authority?

Yes. Blue Logistics operates under MC 1689495, and the authority verification link below opens the public FMCSA record.

How do existing customers track shipments?

Existing customers can use the Customer Portal for available tracking information, shipment activity and customer documents.
